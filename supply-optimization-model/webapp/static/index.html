Supply planning

Plan the season, one area at a time

Every year starts from a locked base model. You make your own working model on top of it, change what you want, and run it. Nobody else can change your working model, and nobody can change the base.

    Locked, one set per contract year

    Base models

    A base model is every area's inputs for a contract year: contracts, hub prices, differentials, demand, freight and storage. It can't be changed once it's made. Working models are built on top of it.

    Make next year's base

    The same way the Excel baselines were made: keep the contracts, differentials, prices and freight, and bring in the new year's demand. No Excel needed.

    Read a base from the Excel models

    For when the area workbooks in the Model folder have been updated and you want them in the app as a new locked base.

    Side by side

    Compare

    Run two models for a region and see where the cost moves. Either side can be a base or anybody's working model.

    Quick demand change

    Three clicks: pick the months, pick where, pick how much. You see exactly what changes before anything is saved.

    Detailed: % of budget demand by state and month

    The base demand is the budget demand times these percentages (the "State Adjustments" sheet). Change a percentage and every plant in that state is scaled to match, in this working model only. The quick change above fills these in for you.

    Price sensitivity

    Move each hub price, and freight, up and down and see what the season costs. Nothing is saved.

    Cold-winter stress test

    Add demand in the months you pick and see whether supply keeps up, and what it costs. Nothing is saved.

    Build optimization model

    Resize this area's model. Slide up to add supply points (new contracts), plants or price hubs, then name them below and press Build. The base model is never changed.

    Change log

    Everything this working model changes from the base, newest first.

    Who's working?

    Your name decides which working models are yours. Only you can change yours; everybody else can look at them and make their own copy. (There's no password yet - this is a local test version.)

    Upload a workbook

    Worked on an area's workbook offline? Upload it to see it in the app: inputs, results and what changed against the base model. It becomes a new working model of yours, so nothing else is touched.
